Game: guess the kappa

Look at the table, estimate kappa in your head, then check. Within 0.10 counts as a hit.

What to notice

  • Load “Two coin-flipping raters”: they agree half the time, yet kappa is 0. All of their agreement is what chance alone would produce.
  • Load “A rare finding”: the physicians agree on 92% of patients, but kappa is low, because when almost everyone is “no”, chance agreement is already very high.
  • Kappa = (observed agreement − chance agreement) ÷ (1 − chance agreement).
