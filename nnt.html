What to notice

  • Compare “Office hour example” with “Rare outcome, same relative risk”: the relative risk is 0.75 in both, but you need to treat 20 people in one case and 200 in the other to prevent one event.
  • NNT = 1 ÷ (risk in untreated − risk in treated), rounded up to the next whole number. If treatment raises the risk, the same calculation gives the number needed to harm (NNH).
  • NNT speaks to practitioners, but it should be reported with a 95% confidence interval and does not capture quality of life.
