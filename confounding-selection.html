What to notice

  • Confounding: a third factor associated with both exposure and outcome. Randomization balances confounders, including unmeasured ones; in observational studies, stratification, matching or adjustment control measured confounders. Notice how the within-stratum RR stays 1.00 while the crude RR moves.
  • Selection bias is about how we select or retain participants: self-selection, control selection bias, loss to follow-up. It can arise even in a perfectly randomized trial, and adjusting the analysis afterwards cannot easily fix it.
  • Information bias is about how we gather information or measurements, for example recall bias or observer bias.
