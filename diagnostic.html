What to notice

  • Moving the cutoff to the left catches more cases (sensitivity up) but mislabels more healthy people (specificity down). There is no cutoff that improves both.
  • Sensitivity and specificity are properties of the test. Predictive values also depend on how common the disease is in the people tested.
  • Try prevalence 1%: even a good test gives mostly false positives. This is why screening a low-risk population produces many false alarms, and why the same test performs better in a high-risk clinic.
